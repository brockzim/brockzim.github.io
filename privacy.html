Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Channel Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool run by Pedro Henrique de Jesus to manage his own YouTube channels. It has no public users.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google OAuth, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's channels and set their title, description, tags and publish date;
  • list the owner's videos, playlists and their status (to avoid duplicate uploads and find free publishing dates);
  • read public video statistics and public comments, and read YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channels.

How data is stored and used

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. They are never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • Statistics and comments are saved locally to help the owner plan better content. They are not sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • The app does not use the data for advertising and does not combine it with other sources about individuals.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions. Locally stored data is deleted on request.
